## How to navigate

- Start at a domain hub. Each hub has an overview, its key concepts, and a catalog of every
  concept, entity and synthesis page in that domain.
- Pages live in shared folders (`wiki/concepts/`, `wiki/entities/`, `wiki/syntheses/`), so one page
  can belong to several domains and appear in several hubs.
- Every claim links to a source summary in `wiki/sources/`, which links to the untouched original
  in `raw/`.
- New material arrives in [[wiki/_compile-queue]] and in the daily digest (`digests/daily/`).
  Compile sessions turn it into pages; see `CLAUDE.md` at the vault root.
- Operations history: [[wiki/log]].
- In Obsidian: open the graph for the concept network (raw sources and digests are filtered out);
  search `path:wiki/concepts OR path:wiki/entities` to see only ideas and entities.
